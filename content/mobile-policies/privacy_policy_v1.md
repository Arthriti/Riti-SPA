# Riti Mobile Privacy Policy

Version: **privacy-v1**. Effective date: **17 November 2026**.

Riti: Money & Goals is a mobile app for personal and household financial
planning. This policy covers the app and related account and support services.
The first release is free, offered in India, and intended for account holders
aged 18 or over. There is no payment or subscription module.

Riti is operated by **Amritansh**, an individual using the Riti brand.
The privacy contact and Grievance Officer is **Amritansh**, available at
[privacy@nowriti.app](mailto:privacy@nowriti.app). For app help, write to
[support@nowriti.app](mailto:support@nowriti.app). You may write in English or
Hindi; replies may be in English.

## Information and purposes

- **Account and sign-in:** account identifier, email address, session and
  authentication information. Riti supports email/password, email-code and
  Google sign-in through Supabase. Google sign-in shares the identity and
  account information needed for that sign-in. Riti does not ask for your Google
  password. Sign-in providers handle credentials under their own policies.
- **Household financial records:** information you enter or import about income,
  expenses, accounts, balances, budgets, assets, debts, repayments, transfers,
  recurring schedules, goals, dates, categories, notes and planning assumptions.
  The app uses these records on your device to organize finances and calculate
  household views and plans.
- **People:** names, birth years, gender and earning-member status where entered,
  and links between people and financial records. A Person Profile is distinct
  from a signed-in member with permission to access a household.
- **Access and sync metadata:** household identifiers, membership and roles,
  registered-device identifiers, public security keys, approval/join records,
  delivery and acknowledgement records, expiry information and account-lifecycle
  results. These support access control and encrypted delivery. Public keys are
  not the private keys used to decrypt household financial content.
- **Agreement evidence:** the account reference, document version, whether the
  action accepted the Terms or acknowledged this policy, and server timestamp.
  We preserve earlier versions and acceptance history. Acknowledging this
  policy is not consent to every activity it describes.
- **Support and privacy requests:** your email address, messages, attachments you
  choose to send and our replies, used to respond and handle requests.
- **Essential service/security information:** providers may process IP addresses,
  request timestamps, authentication events and technical operation results to
  operate and protect their services. The first release does not enable optional
  app analytics or automatic crash-report uploads.

Information comes from you, members of your household, your chosen sign-in
provider and service activity. An entry about someone does not establish that
they supplied it or agreed to its use. Only enter another person's information
when you have the authority and permission required for that use.

## Storage, encryption and household sharing

Household financial and Person Profile records are stored in an encrypted
database on authorized devices. Calculations run on the device. The sync service
relays encrypted records and may temporarily store encrypted payloads and
transfer objects while delivering them. This is not a promise that all data is
only in transit or that nothing is stored on servers.

The service does not receive the household decryption keys needed to read that
encrypted content. Account, access, delivery, acceptance and support information
are separate and can be readable to the service or its providers. Encryption
does not conceal all metadata or remove responsibilities for handling it.

Members with active household access can view and work with shared household
records; Owners also have membership-management powers. Consider this before
sharing access or entering information about others. Removing access cannot
recall screenshots, exports or copies another person already saved independently.
No system can guarantee absolute security. Protect your phone and credentials,
and report suspected unauthorized access to <privacy@nowriti.app>.

## Permissions, imports and files

Camera access is requested for scanning household-join QR codes. You may decline
it or change permission in phone settings; scanning then will not work.
When you choose files for import, the app reads the files you select. Imported
records become household records and follow the same sharing and sync rules.
Copies you save or provide to other services are also subject to the destination's
controls. Do not assume signing out or uninstalling erases separately saved files.

## Providers and locations

Supabase provides authentication, access management and encrypted sync delivery.
Resend handles support and privacy email. These providers may process account,
service/security or email information outside India, including for hosting,
support and recovery copies. This is not a promise that all information remains
in India. Provider recovery copies are distinct from live application records.

Support emails are not protected by the app's household encryption. Please do
not email passwords, sign-in codes, recovery keys, identity documents, bank
statements or household backup files.

Policy links open on nowriti.app, hosted on GitHub Pages, in your system browser.
The app also fetches the required policy pages to check their availability and
version before recording agreement. These requests expose ordinary web-request
metadata, such as an IP address, to the host. They do not send household records
or account identifiers in the document URL. The website and any waitlist have a
separate privacy notice.

## Advertising and other disclosure

The first release has no advertising. We do not sell personal data or use
household information to target advertising. Optional analytics, marketing or
new AI processing are not authorized by the agreement checkbox; any required
separate choice must be presented separately before that use begins.

We may disclose information we actually hold where legally required or lawfully
necessary to investigate misuse, protect the service, or establish or defend
legal claims. Disclosure is limited to the relevant purpose. It does not give
us the ability to decrypt household content we cannot otherwise read.

## Retention and deletion

Household records on phones remain available for household use until the
app's applicable removal/cleanup action or the user removes their local copy.
Archival or access restriction is not erasure. Shared records and independently
saved files require separate consideration when handling a request.

Encrypted sync payloads and transfer objects have temporary delivery and expiry
lifecycles. Delivery/control records, authentication information, essential logs
and provider recovery copies have different lifecycles. Essential logs are used
for service operation, security investigations and applicable legal retention;
provider recovery copies follow the relevant recovery cycle. They do not all disappear
when an encrypted payload expires. A deletion response will explain relevant
information retained and its purpose rather than claim simultaneous erasure of
every provider or user-controlled copy.

For ordinary resolved support enquiries, we move the conversation to Trash;
the email provider schedules deletion 30 days later. For privacy requests, we
keep only the necessary closure confirmation in the existing inbox for three
years after final closure. After checking that record and any applicable hold,
we Trash unnecessary message bodies and attachments. Archiving is not deletion.

Minimal Terms acceptance and Privacy acknowledgement history is retained while
the account is active, then for three years after account deletion. It contains
the restricted account reference, versions, actions and timestamps, not household
financial content. A specific legal obligation or documented hold may extend
retention for the necessary records. Records are deleted when that requirement
and the ordinary period have ended. Provider recovery copies may expire later.

## Complaints

Email [privacy@nowriti.app](mailto:privacy@nowriti.app) with a short description
of your concern and the outcome you are requesting. Amritansh, Riti's operator
and Grievance Officer, handles complaints. Include only information needed to
understand the issue. We may request proportionate verification before acting
on account-specific requests. We will respond by email with the outcome or
necessary next steps, subject to applicable legal deadlines. You may follow up
in the same email thread. This process does not limit access to statutory
complaint routes or other legal remedies.

## Requests, correction and withdrawal

Email [privacy@nowriti.app](mailto:privacy@nowriti.app) to ask about information,
request access, correction, retrieval or deletion, raise a grievance, or withdraw
consent where a particular use depends on it. These routes remain available if
you decline a policy update or no longer have the app. We cannot reconstruct
or decrypt household records from the sync service on your behalf.

For an emailed account-deletion request, we send a fresh confirmation to the
registered account email and require an explicit, request-bound reply. A matching
sender address alone is not sufficient. If you have lost access, use an available
authenticated route or your sign-in/email provider's recovery process. Unresolved
cases receive individual review; routine identity-document collection is not
required by this procedure. Do not send sensitive documents with the first email.

We will explain the verification, scope, remaining information and next steps.
We do not describe pending or blocked deletion as completed. Signing out or
uninstalling is not an account-deletion request. Withdrawing a particular consent
does not by itself delete an account; we will explain its effect on the relevant
use or feature. Applicable rights and complaint routes are preserved.

## Children and other people's profiles

Children cannot create an account under Riti's adult-only account rule. An adult
may enter information about a child or dependant in household planning; this is
different from giving that person account access. Terms acceptance and Privacy
acknowledgement do not constitute child-specific parental consent or verify
adulthood, parenthood or legal guardianship. Contact <privacy@nowriti.app> about
unauthorized entries, withdrawal or deletion involving a child's information.

## Changes and languages

We identify policies by version and effective date. For material changes we
notify affected account holders by email and in the app before they take effect,
unless an immediate legal or security change requires earlier action. In that
case we notify as soon as reasonably possible. Where fresh consent is required,
notice or an acknowledgement does not replace it.

Required updates must be explicitly acknowledged online before ordinary use
continues once detected. Offline devices cannot immediately discover a new
requirement. Policy access, sign-out and available data-rights routes remain
available. English and Hindi versions are intended to have equivalent meaning;
neither automatically overrides the other. Tell us about discrepancies so we
can correct them while preserving your applicable rights.
