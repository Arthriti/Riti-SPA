Website notice

Privacy, explained plainly.

This notice covers the public Riti website and its email-based beta waitlist. It does not describe data handling inside the Riti mobile app.

Last updated: 3 October 2026

What this website collects

This website does not collect or store waitlist entries. When you select a waitlist link, your device opens a pre-addressed draft in your email app. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send that email.

Why the email is used

If you send the email, your address and anything you include in the message are used only to manage Riti beta access and related communication. Do not include financial records, account credentials, identity documents, or other sensitive information.

Hosting and technical information

The website is designed for GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process ordinary request information needed to deliver and protect the site, such as IP address, browser details, requested page, and request time, under its own privacy terms.

Analytics and recording

This site’s source does not include product analytics, advertising trackers, cookies, or session-replay software. If that changes, this notice must be updated before the new collection begins.

Email delivery

If you send a waitlist request, your email provider and the recipient’s email provider process the message under their own terms and privacy notices. The website does not send the message or receive a confirmation from those services.

Retention and deletion

Waitlist emails are kept only while beta-access communication remains relevant or until you ask for removal, subject to any limited retention required for security, legal, or operational reasons.

Your choices

You may ask to confirm, correct, or remove your waitlist email by contacting Riti.
