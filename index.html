01 / Why Riti

Your financial life is always changing.

Salaries change, families grow, goals evolve, parents retire, homes are purchased, and unexpected events happen. Yet most financial plans remain static—or fragmented across different tools.

02 / How it works

One evolving picture of your household.

Your Financial Twin is a living picture of what your household earns, spends, owns, owes, and plans. As life changes, the picture can evolve with it.

04 / Our approach

Clarity before complexity.

  1. Reliable by design. Calculations should be deterministic and explainable.
  2. AI with boundaries. AI may explain and help explore possibilities, but it must not invent financial truth.
  3. Useful from the start. Begin with what you know and receive better guidance as context grows.
  4. Household native. Plan around shared responsibilities, not isolated individual decisions.
  5. People stay in control. Meaningful financial decisions always remain with the household.

06 / Questions, answered

Good things to know upfront.

What is Riti?

Riti is an India-first mobile app designed to help households understand and plan their financial lives as one evolving picture.

Is Riti available now?

Riti is currently in beta, with access being introduced gradually through the waitlist.

Is Riti only an expense tracker?

No. Everyday money is one part of a broader direction covering household position, plans, goals, and decision support.

Does Riti make financial decisions for me?

No. Riti is designed to explain information, assumptions, and possibilities while leaving meaningful decisions with your household.
