Questions, answered

Good things to know upfront.

What is Riti?

Riti is an India-first phone app designed to help individuals, couples, and households understand change and plan their financial lives together.

Is Riti available now?

Riti is in private beta, with access being introduced gradually through the waitlist.

Is Riti only an expense tracker?

No. Everyday money is one part of a broader direction that connects household position, emergency reserves, responsibilities, goals, and decisions.

Does Riti make financial decisions for me?

No. Riti is designed to explain information, assumptions, and possibilities while leaving meaningful decisions with your household.

What happens when I join the waitlist?

Your email app opens with a prepared request. You join only after you choose to send that message to the Riti team.
